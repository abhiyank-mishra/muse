## 🌟 Overview

**Muse Music** is a fast, lightweight, and modern Android streaming music player designed to give you a distraction-free music experience with zero ads. Stream millions of tracks, build playlists, explore trending charts, and track your daily listening habits effortlessly.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎶 **Ad-Free Streaming** – Enjoy uninterrupted music playback with zero interruptions.
- 🔥 **Daily Listening Streak & Counter** – Build your daily streak and keep count of your daily tracks.
- 🌙 **Modern Dark Aesthetics** – Sleek, eye-friendly user interface with smooth animations and transitions.
- 🎧 **Background & Lock Screen Playback** – Seamless audio in the background with lock screen controls and custom Muse notification banner.
- 👥 **Listen Together** – Connect and listen to music synchronously with friends.
- ⚡ **Native Android Performance** – Crafted with Kotlin & Jetpack Compose for peak speed and battery efficiency.
